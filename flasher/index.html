HydroTemp

Install the firmware

One button, no tools. The device then sets itself up from your phone.

You need
  • An ESP32 board with a WROOM-32 module and 4 MB of flash, such as the ESP32 DevKit V1 with a CH340 or CP2102 USB chip.
  • A 0.96" SSD1306 OLED, 128×64, on I2C: SDA to GPIO22, SCL to GPIO21.
  • Two DS18B20 probes on one data line to GPIO4 with a 4.7 kΩ pull-up to VCC. The probes take power from the board: VCC to GPIO16, GND to GPIO2.
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer. Firefox and Safari cannot talk to serial ports.
Install
  1. Connect the board's USB port to the computer.
  2. Press the button, pick the serial port in the dialog, confirm. A fresh board takes about a minute.
This browser cannot access serial ports. Use Chrome or Edge, or flash with esptool below. Serial access needs HTTPS. Open this page over https://. Reinstalling keeps your settings, sensor binding and history unless you choose "erase".
Then
  1. The OLED shows four hex digits and 192.168.4.1. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network HydroTemp-XXXX with those digits. The setup page opens by itself; if not, open http://192.168.4.1/.
  2. Pick your home Wi-Fi, enter the password, save. The device restarts and is reachable at http://hydrotemp-xxxx.local/ or at the address your router gave it.
  3. The probes are bound to the slots left and right in the order they are found. Rename them and set their ranges on the Settings page.
If the port does not show up
  • Windows may need the CH340 or CP210x driver from the chip vendor; macOS and Linux have them built in.
  • Use a data cable, not a charge-only one.
  • Close other programs that may hold the port, such as a serial monitor.
  • If the flasher cannot connect, hold the board's BOOT button while it starts.
Without a browser

The same image, hydrotemp.bin, flashed with esptool:

esptool --chip esp32 -p PORT -b 460800 write-flash 0x0 hydrotemp.bin

Or with Espressif Flash Download Tool on Windows: chip ESP32, file hydrotemp.bin at address 0x0, SPI mode DIO.
